# About the Agentic System

**BUS 4498 Team Build**

> **Problem to be solved**: Cal Poly club members often pay for event supplies out of pocket and then submit reimbursement requests through their club treasurer. Requests are frequently sent back because a receipt is missing, the expense is in the wrong category, or the purchase breaks a funding rule, such as having no itemized receipt or not getting approval in advance. As a rough baseline, about 35% of requests are returned at least once, which can delay repayment by weeks and leaves treasurers checking every request by hand.

### System Goal

For Cal Poly club members who pay out of pocket and the treasurers who process their requests, get reimbursement requests right the first time, measured by the share of requests returned at least once moving from about 35% to 10% or less, without approving or issuing any payment on its own or storing members' bank or payment details.

### Who Is Better Off When This Works?

Club members get paid back sooner, and treasurers spend less time chasing missing receipts, because requests are checked against the funding rules before they are submitted.
